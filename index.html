01 / WHO WE ARE

NOT JUST ONE
THING. EVERYTHING.

We bring ambitious ideas to life across games, software, digital products and the communities around them.

A home for curious minds and unreasonably big ideas.
Mythical Studios is an independent creative technology brand built around one simple belief: the best work happens when imagination and execution share the same room. We experiment, design, develop and ship projects across different corners of the digital world. Some are big launches. Some start as late-night ideas. All of them move us forward.

We are not here to fit into one category. We are here to make useful, memorable things — and have fun building them.

09 / HOW WE BUILD

FROM FIRST
THOUGHT TO LAUNCH.

Big ideas become real through small, deliberate steps. Our approach keeps creativity moving without losing sight of the details.

STEP 01 — DISCOVER

Find the spark.

Start with the problem, the audience and the reason the idea should exist. Get clear on what matters.

STEP 02 — CREATE

Make it tangible.

Design, prototype and build the experience. Test assumptions early and improve what isn't working.

STEP 03 — RELEASE

Put it out there.

Polish the details, share the work and learn from the people who use it. Then make it better.
