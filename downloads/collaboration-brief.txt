MYTHICAL STUDIOS — COLLABORATION BRIEF

Thanks for your interest in collaborating.

When reaching out, please include:
1. Your name and the best way to contact you.
2. A short introduction and relevant experience.
3. The project or idea you want to discuss.
4. What you hope to contribute or achieve.
5. Relevant links, examples or portfolio work.
6. Any timeline or constraints we should know about.

We review proposals based on fit, clarity and current project priorities. Sending a proposal does not guarantee acceptance or a paid role.

Contact: use the contact page on the Mythical Studios website.
